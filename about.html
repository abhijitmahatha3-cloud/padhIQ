THE ORIGIN

Why we built this.

Growing up in Bokaro, Jharkhand, we saw the same problem everywhere around us — brilliant students falling behind not because they lacked talent, but because quality study resources cost money their families didn't have. Coaching centres charge ₹50,000 a year. Good books aren't always available. And when you have a doubt at 11 PM before your board exam, there's nobody to ask.

Abhijit, a Class 12 student himself, decided to do something about it. Armed with just a laptop and determination, he started building padhIQ — a platform that combines free NCERT resources, an AI tutor available 24/7, and a local marketplace where students can buy and sell study material at fair prices.

The name Project AAA came naturally — it stands for the three of us. Aditya, Arup, and Abhijit — three brothers who believe education should be a right, not a privilege. The younger two (Class 7 and Class 8) became the first beta testers, pointing out every bug, every confusing UI, every missing topic.

This isn't a startup. It isn't a business. It's a student's promise to other students — that no one should have to study alone, or struggle because they couldn't afford help. 🇮🇳

JOURNEY

How it happened.

// THE PROBLEM
Realised the gap — quality education in Bokaro was locked behind expensive coaching fees. Most students couldn't afford it.
// THE IDEA
Started sketching padhIQ — a free platform with NCERT notes, AI doubt-solving, and a local marketplace. No coaching needed.
// THE BUILD
Built the entire site solo — cosmic dark theme, AI chat, marketplace, login system. Every line of code written between study sessions.
// THE LAUNCH
Deployed on Vercel — live at padh-iq.vercel.app. Free for every student in India, powered by Groq AI and Supabase.
// THE FUTURE
Growing padhIQ — more subjects, video explanations, quiz mode, student profiles, and eventually reaching every corner of India. 🇮🇳
